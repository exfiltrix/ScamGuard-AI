# 🔒 Privacy Policy - ScamGuard AI

**Last updated:** April 11, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

## 📋 Introduction

ScamGuard AI ("we", "our service") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

**Core principle:** We analyze only the content that you explicitly send to us for review. We do not read your private conversations outside the material you submit.

---

## ✅ What We Collect

### 1. Listing or Message Data
When you send a link, text, or other content for analysis, we may collect:
- 📝 **Submitted text** such as descriptions, titles, or suspicious messages
- 📸 **Submitted images** for visual analysis
- 🔗 **Submitted URL** if you send a link
- 💰 **Price information** if it is part of the content being analyzed

### 2. Technical Data
- 🆔 **User ID** such as Telegram ID or session identifier
- ⏰ **Request time** for logging and statistics
- 🌐 **IP address** when required for API operation
- 📊 **Analysis results** such as risk score, flags, and recommendations

### 3. Optional Feedback Data
If you submit feedback, we may collect:
- ✅ Your judgment about whether the case was a scam
- 💬 Optional comments you include

---

## ❌ What We Do Not Collect

We do not intentionally collect:
- ❌ Your private messenger conversations beyond what you submit for analysis
- ❌ Contacts from your address book
- ❌ Full Telegram message history
- ❌ Financial credentials such as card numbers or bank account credentials
- ❌ Precise geolocation
- ❌ Authentication secrets such as passwords or tokens

**We analyze only what you deliberately send to the system.**

## 🗄️ How We Store Data

### Retention Periods
| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| Submitted text | 30 days | Re-analysis and feedback handling |
| Submitted images | 30 days | Visual analysis |
| Analysis results | 90 days | User history and quality review |
| Anonymous statistics | Indefinite | Model and product improvement |

### Automatic Deletion
After the retention period ends, data should be automatically deleted.

### Security
- 🔐 **Encryption** for data in transit where supported, such as HTTPS
- 🛡️ **Access controls** for authorized systems and staff only
- 🗄️ **Backups** with limited retention
- 🔒 **Isolation** between user records where supported by the storage layer

---

## 👥 Who Can Access Data

### Internal Access
- **AI models** for automated analysis
- **Developers** for debugging and improvement, ideally using minimized or anonymized data
- **Support staff** when needed to respond to your explicit request

### Third Parties
We do not sell your data. We may send limited content to service providers only when required to operate the product, for example:
- 🤖 **OpenAI / Google Gemini** for AI analysis
- 🛡️ **Cloudflare or similar providers** for infrastructure protection and traffic handling

Where possible, external services should meet recognized security standards.

## 🔧 Your Rights

### 1. Right of Access
You may request a copy of your stored data.

Example:
```text
/data_export in the Telegram bot
→ Receive a JSON file with your history
```

### 2. Right to Deletion
You may request deletion of your stored data.

Example:
```text
/delete_my_data in the Telegram bot
→ Your stored data is permanently deleted
```

### 3. Right to Correction
If an analysis is inaccurate, you may send feedback so the system can be improved.

Example:
```text
/feedback on a specific analysis
→ Helps us review and improve the system
```

### 4. Right to Opt Out of History
If supported, you may disable stored history while still using the analysis flow.

Example:
```text
/privacy_settings → Disable history
→ Analysis still works, but no history is stored
```
