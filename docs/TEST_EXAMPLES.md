# 🧪 Demo Test Examples

## High Risk Examples

### Example 1: Classic Scam
```text
URL: https://t.me/test_channel/scam1

Title: URGENT! Apartment in central Tashkent!

Description:
2-room apartment in central Tashkent. Very cheap - only 800,000 UZS!
Today only. Last chance.

100% card prepayment required. No viewing. No passport required.
WhatsApp only: +998901234567

Expected red flags:
- price far below market
- urgency and pressure
- full prepayment without viewing
- weak identity checks
- messenger-only contact
```

### Example 2: Fake Documents
```text
Luxury apartment at a super price.
Immediate card transfer required.
No contract.
Documents will be sent later.
Stock photos.
```

## Medium Risk Examples

### Example 3: Incomplete Information
```text
Long-term rental in Yakkasaray district.
2 rooms, good renovation.
Price negotiable.
One month prepayment.
```

### Example 4: Suspicious But Possibly Legitimate
```text
Reasonable-looking listing with weak details,
limited trust signals, but no obvious hard scam trigger.
```

## Low Risk Examples

### Example 5: Legitimate Listing Style
```text
3-room apartment, 75 m², official contract, documents available,
clear price, clear contact, in-person viewing allowed.
```

### Example 6: Ordinary Friendly Message
```text
Hi, how are you? Long time no see.
```

## What To Verify

For each example, check:
- final risk score
- risk level
- quality of red flags
- whether recommendations fit the case
